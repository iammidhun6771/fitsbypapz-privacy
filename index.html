FITSBY.PAPZ Privacy Policy Effective date: 8 November 2026 1. About this application FITSBY.PAPZ is a Pinterest content management and publishing application operated by the FITSBY.PAPZ account owner ("we", "us", or "our"). It is intended to help manage our own Pinterest presence, including preparing and publishing Pins, organizing content into boards, and reviewing account or Pin performance information where the Pinterest API makes that information available. This application is not currently offered as a public service for other users to connect their Pinterest accounts. 2. Information accessed When we authorize the application through Pinterest OAuth, it may access only the information permitted by the scopes we approve. Depending on the enabled features, this may include: Pinterest account identifiers and basic account information; board names, identifiers, and board-related information; Pin content and identifiers, including information needed to create or manage Pins; analytics or performance information made available through the approved API permissions; and OAuth access or refresh tokens needed to authenticate API requests. We do not ask for or collect a Pinterest account password. Authentication is handled through Pinterest's authorization process. 3. How information is used Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to operate the features described in this policy, such as preparing and publishing selected content, organizing Pins into boards, and reviewing permitted analytics. We do not sell Pinterest data, use it to build profiles for unrelated purposes, or share it with advertising networks or data brokers. Any Pin or board action must be deliberately selected by the account operator. The application is not intended to generate unsolicited engagement, such as automatically following accounts, commenting, or sending messages. 4. Storage, security, and retention Pinterest information is accessed through the Pinterest API when needed. We do not intentionally retain Pinterest API data beyond what is necessary to provide the application features and what Pinterest's terms expressly permit. If permitted analytics information is retained, it is used only for the authorized account and purpose. OAuth tokens are used only when needed to authenticate requests to Pinterest. Access to credentials is limited to the application operator, and credentials should not be exposed in public repositories or shared with unauthorized people. We take reasonable steps to protect credentials and information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. 5. Sharing and third-party services We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared with Pinterest only as necessary for the application to make API requests and perform actions authorized by the account owner. Pinterest's own handling of information is governed by its policies and terms. If the application is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, that provider may process limited technical information needed to run the application. Any provider used must be identified and covered by appropriate privacy and security terms. 6. Data deletion and revoking access The account owner can revoke the application's access through Pinterest's connected-app or account security settings. When access is revoked, the application will stop making authenticated requests. The operator will delete stored Pinterest data and credentials when they are no longer needed, when deletion is requested, or when required by Pinterest's terms or applicable law. For a deletion or privacy request, contact us using the email address in Section 9. We will respond within a reasonable period and handle Pinterest data in accordance with applicable requirements. Pinterest's developer terms may require deletion of Pinterest data within specified time limits. 7. Your choices You can choose not to authorize the application or can revoke authorization at any time through Pinterest. You may also contact us to ask about information associated with the application or request deletion, subject to applicable law and Pinterest's requirements. 8. Children's privacy This application is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. 9.
